Speed-to-Lead AI Workflow

An n8n workflow that qualifies inbound enquiries with an LLM, creates contacts and deals in Zoho CRM, routes them and triggers follow-up automatically — with human review built in.

  • n8n + LLMAutomated enquiry qualification and routing
  • Zoho CRMContacts and deals created automatically
  • Human reviewValidation, training and handover included

The challenge

SME enquiries were arriving faster than the team could qualify and respond to them. Slow, manual follow-up meant good leads went cold and CRM records were incomplete.

Objective

Respond to every enquiry quickly and consistently, with qualified leads landing in the CRM ready for sales.

Approach

  1. Assess

    Mapped the customer journey, enquiry sources and CRM requirements with the business owner.

  2. Build

    Linked n8n, APIs and webhooks to capture enquiries and connect them to Zoho CRM.

  3. Qualify

    Added LLM-based qualification that scores and routes each enquiry, then creates contacts and deals and triggers follow-up.

  4. Hand over

    Built in validation and human review, then trained the team and handed the workflow over.

Outcomes

Enquiries are captured, qualified, logged and followed up automatically, with a human checkpoint where it matters — so the team spends time on qualified conversations instead of admin.
