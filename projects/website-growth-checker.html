Website Growth Checker

Website audits, evidence-based scoring and prioritised recommendations.

Business challenge

Small businesses need to identify actionable content, technical and conversion problems without relying on unexplained website scores.

Commercial objective

Turn website diagnostics into a repeatable, evidence-led business-growth product, with a pathway from free checks to paid reporting.

My contribution

Built a Next.js audit interface with Python/FastAPI crawling and scoring, Supabase-backed reporting, and a documented £29 Pro upgrade flow. The repository describes 30 deterministic checks in 10 areas, evidence-linked results, expanded Pro checks and PDF output.

Delivery & outcomes

Delivered a working product implementation for scalable website diagnostics and an upgrade pathway. The repository documents features, not verified paid-customer numbers or revenue.

Capabilities & technologies

Next.js · Python · FastAPI · Supabase · SEO and conversion analytics · Stripe
