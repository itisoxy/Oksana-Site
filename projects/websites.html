Website Design & Build

Websites created and managed across Webflow, Figma, WordPress, HubSpot and Umbraco — combining front-end development, SaaS interfaces and AI to launch fast, credible sites for growing businesses.

  • 8+Sites shown across sectors
  • 5 platformsWebflow · Figma · WordPress · HubSpot · Umbraco
  • Up to 40%Client sales growth from Braganda web projects

The challenge

Small and growing businesses need a site that wins trust and turns visitors into enquiries — without a long agency project or an in-house team to run it.

Objective

Deliver professional, conversion-focused websites quickly, on platforms the client can manage, with clear calls to action and lead capture built in.

Approach

  1. Design

    Designed layouts and brand presentation in Figma around each business’s services and proof points.

  2. Build

    Built on the right platform for the client — Webflow, WordPress, HubSpot or Umbraco — with front-end development where needed.

  3. Convert

    Added clear quote and contact pathways, trust signals and enquiry capture.

  4. Launch & manage

    Launched, managed and iterated the sites, using AI to speed up content and build.

Outcomes

A portfolio of live sites across construction, home improvement, recruitment, healthcare and payments. Braganda’s web and digital marketing projects helped clients grow sales by up to 40%.
