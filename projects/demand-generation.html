Global Processing Services (now Thredd) · Payments

Demand Generation: Paid, Social & Digital Events

Multi-channel demand generation for a B2B payments platform — paid search, paid social, LinkedIn and virtual events — measured against pipeline, not just clicks.

  • £233kPipeline from Google PPC campaigns
  • +20%Website visitors from PPC
  • +550%LinkedIn followers in one year
  • 9xPaid social exposure at 0.6–0.9% CTR

The challenge

A specialist B2B payments brand needed to reach fintech decision-makers globally, build awareness in a crowded market and turn digital attention into qualified pipeline.

Objective

Grow reach and qualified demand across search, social and events, with every channel tracked through to pipeline.

Approach

  1. Paid search

    Led targeted Google PPC campaigns, optimising for qualified traffic and pipeline value.

  2. Paid social

    Developed paid social campaigns that achieved 9x exposure with a 0.6–0.9% click-through rate.

  3. Organic social

    Grew the LinkedIn audience by 550% in one year.

  4. Digital events

    Managed digital events for up to 200 guests on Remo and Brella, connected to CRM follow-up.

Outcomes

Google PPC delivered a 20% increase in visitors and £233,000 of pipeline. LinkedIn followers grew by 550% in a year, paid social achieved 9x exposure at a 0.6–0.9% CTR, and digital events reached up to 200 guests each.
