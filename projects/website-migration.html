Three-Stage Website Migration

Owned the web roadmap through three transformations ahead of a funding round: WordPress to HubSpot, a HubSpot refresh and rebrand, then a full redesign and migration to Umbraco.

  • 3 stagesWordPress → HubSpot → rebrand → Umbraco
  • Sole marketerLed as the only member of the marketing team
  • Funding-readyUmbraco relaunch delivered ahead of the funding round

The challenge

The website had to keep pace with a fast-growing payments business: new products, a new brand and investor scrutiny. Every change touched Product, Engineering, Sales and regional teams, and lead capture had to keep flowing into the CRM throughout.

Objective

Move the site onto the right platform at each stage of growth without losing leads, search visibility or stakeholder confidence.

Approach

  1. WordPress → HubSpot

    Led the first migration from WordPress to HubSpot, bringing web pages, forms and lead capture into the marketing platform.

  2. Refresh & rebrand

    Delivered a refresh and rebrand on HubSpot as the business repositioned.

  3. Redesign → Umbraco

    Led the redesign and migration to Umbraco ahead of the funding round, with a small technical implementation team.

  4. Align & connect

    Brought Product, Engineering, Sales and regional teams through each stage, and kept forms and lead capture connected to Pardot and Salesforce via APIs and webhooks.

Outcomes

Three platform moves delivered in sequence, with stakeholders aligned on requirements, decisions and rollout at each step. The business went into its funding round with a redesigned site on Umbraco and lead capture still connected to the CRM.
