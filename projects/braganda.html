Independent Venture

Braganda — Founder & Builder

Digital marketing, AI automation and product development.

Business challenge

Small businesses often need a joined-up approach to websites, customer acquisition, enquiry handling and accessible technology tools without a large internal digital team.

Commercial objective

Create and develop an independent digital business serving SME growth needs while testing repeatable product and automation offerings alongside bespoke services.

My contribution

Founded Braganda at 19 and expanded the venture from marketing, branding and website services into three areas: Braganda Agency (digital experience and growth), Braganda Systems (GTM automation and workflow solutions) and Braganda Lab (digital products, learning resources and tools).

Delivery & outcomes

Established a multi-offering venture and developed tangible service propositions, workflow demonstrations and product assets. The cited portfolio/repositories confirm the builds and business model, but do not verify a consolidated revenue or customer-acquisition result for this case study.
