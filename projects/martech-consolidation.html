MarTech Stack Consolidation

Two stages of stack consolidation at a scaling payments business: first bringing Mailchimp, Canva and WordPress workflows into a HubSpot-led setup, then moving automation to Pardot integrated with Salesforce for unified analytics and clearer lead tracking.

  • 2 stagesStack consolidation across GPS and Thredd
  • HubSpot-ledEmail, web and campaign workflows unified
  • Pardot + SalesforceAutomation integrated for unified analytics
  • Lower costsLess software overlap, licence spend and manual effort

The challenge

Email, design, web and campaign work was spread across disconnected tools. Licences overlapped, campaigns took manual effort to run, and lead data did not reach sales cleanly or consistently.

Objective

Run marketing on fewer, connected platforms: one place for campaigns and leads, clean handover to sales, connected analytics and lower running costs.

Approach

  1. Plan

    Developed a 12-month web and eCRM plan covering integration, security, reporting and team training.

  2. Consolidate into HubSpot

    Streamlined Mailchimp, Canva and WordPress workflows into a HubSpot-led setup; built lead-scoring foundations and connected forms, landing pages, email, event activity, alerts and reporting.

  3. Move to Pardot + Salesforce

    Led migration requirements through rollout, aligning Product, Engineering, Sales and regional teams, and implemented API and webhook connections across the CMS, Pardot and Salesforce.

  4. Retire & adopt

    Retired overlapping tools such as Remo in favour of existing platforms, and trained users as HubSpot Super User.

Outcomes

Marketing moved from scattered point tools to an integrated stack. Automation reduced software overlap, licence costs and manual campaign effort, and the Pardot–Salesforce integration gave unified analytics and clearer lead tracking from first touch to sales follow-up.
