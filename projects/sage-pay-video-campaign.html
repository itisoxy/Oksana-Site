Sage Pay — Video Campaign

Integrated video-led payments campaign supporting pipeline generation with more efficient media spending.

  • £1.56mContributed to pipeline
  • −10%Media budget
  • +5%Traffic and engagement

The challenge

The payments business needed campaign content and media activity that could contribute to commercial pipeline without unnecessary media spend.

Objective

Support qualified pipeline creation while using the campaign budget efficiently.

Approach

Led the integrated video campaign and its supporting content and media as Digital Marketing Campaign Executive at Sage Pay / Opayo. Worked across departments to align teams behind the campaign, which helped secure increased marketing budgets.

Outcomes

The video campaign contributed £1.56m to pipeline. Media budget was cut by 10% while traffic and engagement rose by 5%.
