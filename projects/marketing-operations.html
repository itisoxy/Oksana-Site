Thredd · Payments

Marketing Operations & Platform Enablement

Led the marketing operations backbone of a scaling payments processor through a $400M funding period — integrating platforms, aligning CRM with sales enablement and making the tools stick across the company.

  • $400MFunding period: website reskin & CRM setup delivered via agencies
  • SuperuserCompany-wide training on Pardot, Salesforce & Remo
  • 5 toolsAutomated reporting across HubSpot, Salesforce, Excel, Data Studio & Power BI

The challenge

Rapid growth and new investment meant marketing technology, CRM processes and reporting had to scale quickly. Platforms needed to talk to each other, sales and marketing needed a shared CRM approach, and teams needed confidence using new tools.

Objective

Build marketing operations that could support a larger, faster-moving business: integrated platforms, a CRM strategy aligned to sales enablement, automated reporting and a team trained to use it.

Approach

  1. Integrate

    Integrated Pardot with Salesforce and Remo so campaigns, events and lead data flowed into one system.

  2. Align

    Developed and implemented a CRM strategy aligned with sales enablement, so marketing activity fed the sales process directly.

  3. Automate reporting

    Built automated reporting dashboards across HubSpot, Salesforce, Excel macros, Google Data Studio and Power BI.

  4. Enable & deliver

    Acted as platform superuser, delivered company-wide training, and managed agencies for the website reskin and CRM setup during $400M funding rounds.

Outcomes

The business gained connected marketing and sales platforms, one CRM approach shared by both teams, and reporting that runs automatically instead of by hand. Company-wide training meant the tools were adopted across departments, not just in marketing.
