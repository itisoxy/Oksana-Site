I'm a London-based Marketing Technologist and independent consultant with over 9 years of experience delivering digital transformation initiatives across marketing strategy, technology, data, automation and AI.

My background spans startups, growing businesses and established organisations, including FTSE 100 companies and leading financial technology and payments providers such as Global Processing Services (now Thredd), Sage and Opayo.

Working extensively within financial services, fintech and highly regulated environments, I've developed a multidisciplinary approach that combines commercial strategy with hands-on technical execution. I specialise in designing and orchestrating end-to-end marketing journeys, connecting the different layers of technology, data and automation that underpin customer engagement, acquisition and growth.

My experience spans go-to-market strategy, marketing operations, CRM and systems integration, website development, analytics and business process optimisation. From shaping customer journeys and building automated workflows to integrating platforms and developing performance measurement frameworks, I focus on creating connected digital ecosystems that deliver measurable business value.

As technology continues to evolve, my work increasingly incorporates AI solutions development, intelligent automation and data-driven applications, including retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), LLM integrations, Python, APIs and cloud-based technologies. My technical experience extends to developing, integrating and deploying cloud-based applications.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of AI innovation, responsible technology adoption and governance, exploring how organisations can leverage emerging technologies while maintaining data integrity, transparency, operational resilience and appropriate oversight.

I work with organisations looking to stay ahead of the curve by embracing emerging technologies and AI-driven innovation. Combining strategic thinking with a forward-looking approach to technology, I help bridge the gap between business objectives and practical solutions, from initial discovery and planning through to implementation and delivery.
